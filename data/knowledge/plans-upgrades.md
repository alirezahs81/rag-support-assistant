# Plans and Upgrades

Plans include Free, Pro, and Business. Pro adds higher API limits and priority support. Business adds SSO, audit logs, and dedicated success contact.

You can upgrade instantly from **Account → Plans**. Prorated charges apply for the remaining billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal so you keep paid features until the period ends.

Students and nonprofits can request discounted seats by submitting verification documents to sales support.
