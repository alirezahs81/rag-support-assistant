# Password Reset

To reset your password, open the sign-in page and click **Forgot password**. Enter the email address linked to your account. You will receive a reset link valid for 30 minutes.

If you do not see the email, check spam or promotions folders. You can request a new link after 60 seconds. After resetting, sign in with the new password on all devices.

Security tip: use a unique password and enable two-factor authentication when available.
