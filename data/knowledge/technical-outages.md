# Technical Issues and Outages

Check the status page before opening a ticket. Known outages are listed with estimated recovery times. For app crashes, update to the latest version, clear cache, and retry on a stable network.

Upload failures often come from file size limits (100 MB) or unsupported formats. Compress large files or convert to PDF/PNG when possible.

If the service is down only for you, capture error codes from the browser console and include them in your support ticket for faster diagnosis.
