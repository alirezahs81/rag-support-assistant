# Billing and Refunds

Invoices are issued monthly on your plan anniversary date. You can download invoices from **Account → Billing**. Payments are charged to the card on file.

Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase for annual plans if usage is under the fair-use threshold. To request a refund, open a ticket with the invoice number and reason. Refunds appear on your statement within 5–10 business days.

Duplicate charges: if you were billed twice, contact billing support with both transaction IDs; we reverse the duplicate after verification.
