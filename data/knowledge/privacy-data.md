# Privacy and Data Export

You can export account data from **Account → Privacy → Download my data**. Exports are delivered as a ZIP within 24 hours and include profile, tickets, and billing history.

To delete your account, request deletion under the same menu. Deletion is permanent after a 14-day recovery window. Backups are purged on the following retention cycle.

We do not sell personal data. Processing details are described in the privacy policy linked in the product footer.
